TASTEBYTE RESTAURANT — WEBSITE DESIGNING EXAM PROJECT
====================================================

FILES:
- index.html: complete website
- style.css: responsive styling
- script.js: mobile navigation, menu filters, booking date
- success.html: thank-you page after a Netlify form submission

HOW TO PREVIEW:
Open index.html in Chrome or another browser while connected to the internet.
The website uses online food photos and Google Fonts; these require internet.

HOW TO HOST FOR FREE:
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Sign in or create a Netlify account to keep control of your website.
3. Drag the 'tastebyte-website' FOLDER (containing index.html) into the drop area,
   or upload the ZIP file if supported by the current Netlify UI.
4. Netlify provides a public URL ending in .netlify.app.
5. Open the URL and test menu filters, mobile menu and forms.
6. In Netlify, open your site > Forms and ensure form detection is enabled.
   If needed, enable it and redeploy the files; forms require Netlify hosting.
7. Use Netlify's site/domain settings to choose a site name if desired.
8. Send the actual working URL to your lecturer before the exam.

IMPORTANT:
- Sample menu items/prices, location and hours are placeholders for this exam project.
- No restaurant phone number or email address has been invented.
- Reservation requests ARE NOT instant confirmed bookings. The restaurant must
  review the form entry in Netlify and contact the visitor.
- Contact and reservation forms only store submissions after Netlify deployment
  and Forms activation; they do not submit data on a local file preview.
- If your lecturer requires a paid custom domain, a free .netlify.app subdomain
  may not meet that stricter requirement; confirm the lecturer's instructions.

SUGGESTED EXAM EXPLANATION:
Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript.
Hosting: Netlify.
Functionality: responsive navigation, interactive menu category filters,
reservation-request and contact forms, accessible mobile-friendly layout.
